Our Story

Zimbabwe’s Trusted HD Hyundai Dealer

“Customer satisfaction is the only target to aim at.” — Nicnel’s founding principle

Nicnel Plant & Equipment (Pvt) Ltd was established in 1999, built on a simple belief: supply an incomparable service, unrivaled supply of goods, and undivided attention to our customers — old-fashioned one-on-one dealing backed by modern technical expertise.

As an authorized HD Hyundai dealer, our product list ranges from a small forklift right up to our largest 120-ton excavator. We are specialists in heavy haulage — constantly moving equipment from Durban to destinations across southern Africa — and our plant hire division is supported by skilled managers, operators and a dedicated field service crew.

Today we cover the full spectrum of earthmoving requirements in Zimbabwe and beyond its borders, serving construction, mining, industry, farming and transport clients with Hyundai machinery built for the toughest conditions.
