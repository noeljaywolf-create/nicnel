About Nicnel

Zimbabwe's Authorized HD Hyundai Dealer

Nicnel Plant & Equipment (Pvt) Ltd was founded in 1999 by Nelson Dias. Today our product range spans from a small forklift right up to our 120-ton Hyundai excavator.

We're specialists in heavy haulage — constantly moving equipment from Durban to destinations across southern Africa — backed by a plant hire division with skilled operators, managers and an on-site field service crew.
